Penske Truck Leasing — Data Analytics Internship Summary (May 2025 - Dec 2025)

During my internship at Penske Truck Leasing, I analyzed 12 months of historical telemetry data to design and refine an anomaly detection logic aimed at reducing recurring false alerts. The logic was validated on a 59-day evaluation period, demonstrating the potential to prevent approximately 5,600 false failures, avoid 751 support tickets, and reduce monthly ticket volume by nearly 80%, saving an estimated 22–44 analyst hours per week.

In recognition of the impact and quality of this work, I was selected for an internship extension and given a higher-scope initiative focused on enterprise data quality and reporting. In this stage of my internship, I designed and developed a scalable AWS QuickSight data quality dashboard integrating 21 source tables and 823 attributes via AWS Athena. I built optimized joins, calculated fields, and KPI logic to assess key quality indicators, surface anomalies, and deliver clear, stakeholder-ready insights for both technical and non-technical teams. I collaborated closely with senior data analysts to validate results, document data definitions, and ensure accuracy, transparency, and long-term usability.

Undergraduate Thesis Work Using Python, Tableau, and Excel

I undertook a project over the course of three semesters to analyze the animation industry and its impact on economic development. My research aimed to prove that the animation industry could be used as a tool for economic growth, and to do so, I had to create the dataset myself due to the novel nature of the research. I used data from The World Bank database, The Park Database, and Google Trends to conduct my research. My extensive analysis led me to a significant conclusion that the animation industry has a profound impact on economic development. Moreover, it could serve as a valuable tool for policymakers to target development.
